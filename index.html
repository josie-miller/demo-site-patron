01 / IDENTITY

Sign in. Find your circles.

Supabase Auth manages sign-up and sign-in. The app loads the member’s profile, while Postgres stores museum circles, visits, follows, comments, and reactions.

Read AuthManager.swift ↗
02 / SAVING A VISIT

Keep it on the phone first.

Pending visit records and photos are saved to disk so unfinished uploads can be retried after the app closes. The upload path writes photos to Storage and upserts the visit in Postgres using its stable ID.

Explore the upload queue ↗
03 / PHOTOS & ACCESS

Separate files from records.

Photo references live with the visit record; image files live in a private Storage bucket. The app requests expiring signed URLs. SQL policies define record access and include photo access tied to visible visits.

See the photo access policy ↗
04 / CIRCLECODE

A scan connects the visit.

The app sends a scanned CircleCode ID to a Postgres function that resolves its event. Scan records connect check-ins to visit logs and support the app’s points flow.

Explore CircleCode ↗
